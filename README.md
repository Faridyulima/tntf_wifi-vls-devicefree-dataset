# tntf_wifi-vls-devicefree-dataset
These are Wi-Fi &amp; VLS **device-free dataset** collected at Department of Nuclear Engineering and Engineering Physics, **Universitas Gadjah Mada**. The datasets are designed to support research on indoor localization, with a particular focus on multi-sensor, device-free approaches. Our objective is to develop accurate device-free location estimation methods that leverage complementary characteristics to improve performance. This work can enable niche applications, such as monitoring the well-being of elderly individuals.
# Dataset overview
The dataset comprises Wi-Fi Received Signal Strength Indicator (RSSI) measurements and Visible Light Sensor (VLS) illuminance data collected in three distinct environments: an unobstructed area (5 × 5 m), a typical classroom, and a lobby. The data from the unobstructed area were collected by Refa Rupaksi and Aditya Bagus Krisnawan, while the classroom and lobby datasets were collected by Muhammad Wafa, Yasir Abdul Aziz, Faris Afif Ridyasmara, and Muhammad Nur Aslam Anugerah. All data collection activities were conducted under the supervision of Dr. Dwi Joko Suroso.
